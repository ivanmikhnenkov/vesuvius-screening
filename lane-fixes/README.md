# Fixes to the public PHerc. 0306B screening lane

The PHerc. 0306B First Letters team published the lane that found their line of text
([SirProdigle/pherc0306b-first-letters](https://github.com/SirProdigle/pherc0306b-first-letters), `discovery/`, MIT).
It spiral-fits a band, snaps the windings to m7, flattens, renders and inks every winding, and ranks windows. I ran it
on rented boxes over four bands: PHerc0343 as a positive control, and three bands of PHerc0175A. Those runs hit the
failures in rows 1-5, 8 and 9. Rows 6 and 7 are additions the runs used, and I found row 10 in review.

`patches/` has each change as a unified diff against `discovery/` at 3f60827, with paths as in the repo. The later
commits 9bb6cef and 3b15a16 touch none of the patched files, and the patches apply there too. `scripts/` has the new
helpers, and `../RUN.md` runs the lane with all of it.

| # | problem (as seen) | fix | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VC3D's `latest` release moves. Its baf5e60 (2026-11-03) dropped `vc_render_tifxyz --prefetch-remote`, which the lane passes on every render, and `setup.sh` fell back to `latest`. Seen before a render ran. | `setup.sh` pins the archived c9f8c57 build, with no fallback to `latest`. | Renders of the PHerc0343 First Letters segment are bit-identical to the published ones: the layer and valid-mask sha256 equal the claim's sidecar. |
| 2 | The 0fb38c45 fitter imports `vc3d_fiber_format`, which isn't shipped with `scripts/spiral`. | `scripts/box_prep.sh` puts `vesuvius/src/vc3d_fiber_format` next to the fitter. | The fitter imports and runs. |
| 3 | On an anticlockwise fit (PHerc0175A) the published crossings cache (`*.dbm.crossings.npz`) "could not be remapped ... walk crossing partner is out of range". The exact rebuild then rejected the mirrored coordinates ("track coordinates must lie in [0, 2**20)"), and the fit died. | `erwin_data.sh` no longer fetches the cache. The fitter computes exact crossings natively (7,438,580 events in about 1 min). | Three anticlockwise bands of PHerc0175A fitted to 30,000 steps. |
| 4 | A cut 8.5 GB tracks download passed the data stage. The files came from backgrounded `curl` and a bare `wait`, which returns 0 whatever the children did. The fitter then fell back to the placeholder `.dbm` ("database disk image is malformed"). | `erwin_data.sh` resumes each file until its size equals the server's Content-Length, and fails the stage otherwise. Tracks can come from an rsync mirror first (`TRACKS_MIRROR`). | A truncated `coordinates.i32` (5.97 of 8.48 GB) was completed by range resume, and all 9 files were size-checked. |
| 5 | Triage (`tri_rank.py`) reloaded the winding's full fused map and ran `gap_stats` (a 2 mm blur) over the whole winding for every window. That took 46 min for 40 windings of PHerc0175A on a 24-core host. | The map is cached per winding, and `gap_stats(cols=)` works on the window ± 1000 px. That is more than 4 sigma of the widest blur, and everything outside the window is masked anyway. | Identical output on 6 windows of 3 windings (`a == b` for every returned field), and 2-3 times faster on the same host. In lane runs the triage stage took 2,792 s for 213 windows before the fix, and 1,776 s and 1,444 s for 681 and 680 windows after (other hosts, from the lane's timing logs). |
| 6 | (addition) `window_score.py` computed the row score twice per window and scored windings one at a time. | One `score()` call, reused by the alignment branch, and a `WS_PROCS` process pool over windings (`lane_box.sh` splits the threads across the processes). | Before: not timed. After, with `WS_PROCS=6`: 120 windings were scored while the rest rendered and inked (a 53-67 min post stage), with 112-290 s of scoring left after it (lane timing logs). |
| 7 | (addition) Reader v3 wasn't among the lane's ink models. | Reader v3 in the lane, with its batch capped at 8 for its 384 px tiles. `INK_MODELS` selects models and weights (for example `rv3:1`). | Reader v3 alone inks a winding in a median 4 s of model time (240 windings logged, max 9.5 s). |
| 8 | Results lived only on the rented box, and a box stopped by its host came back wiped. | `scripts/box_sync.sh` copies the durable outputs (fit, meshes, results, logs) off the box every 10 min. It follows links (`out/*/fitted` are symlinks into `fits/`) and checks that the fit checkpoint landed. | After the fix, the fit checkpoint was verified off the box during the run. |
| 9 | 8 of the 20 eligible scrolls have no Lasagna normals, and the lane's `NORMALS=syn` branch called a `make_normals.py` it didn't ship. The authors added theirs in 9bb6cef (2026-11-08). | `../normals/make_normals.py` (CPU), called by the `erwin_data.sh` patch on the bucket's m7. | Three anticlockwise bands of PHerc0175A fitted. The PHerc0343 control, run end to end with these normals, ranks the published text first (`../RUN.md`). |
| 10 | (found in review, not hit in my runs) The post stage's gate compared inked windings with *rendered* ones. `one_winding` returns 0 on a missing snap, a failed flatten or a failed render, so a band with 1 of 120 planned windings rendered and inked passed as complete. | `s_post` counts against the planned windings (`windings.txt`). It writes the planned, rendered and inked counts and the missing windings to `lane.log`, which is packed with the results. A second render pass retries what the first left. The 80 % tolerance stays as upstream had it. | `scripts/test_post_gate.sh` stubs snap, render and ink, with 10 planned windings. With 1 of 10 rendered, the gate passes before the fix (rc 0) and fails after it (rc 1). A render that fails once is retried. Every run I report rendered and inked all its planned windings (40, 120, 120, 40). |

`patches/` covers rows 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10. `lane_box.sh` carries 6 and 10, and `ink_lane.py` and
`fastpipe.py` carry 7.
