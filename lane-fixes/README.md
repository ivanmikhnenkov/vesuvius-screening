# Fixes to the public PHerc. 0306B screening lane

The PHerc. 0306B First Letters team published the lane that found their line
([SirProdigle/pherc0306b-first-letters](https://github.com/SirProdigle/pherc0306b-first-letters), `discovery/`, MIT):
spiral-fit a band, snap the windings to m7, flatten, render, ink every winding, rank windows. Running it on rented
boxes over four bands (PHerc0343 as positive control, three bands of PHerc0175A) hit the failures in rows 1-5, 8 and 9; rows 6 and 7 are additions those runs used,
and row 10 was found in review.
`patches/` holds each change as a unified diff against `discovery/` at 3f60827 (paths as in the repo; the later commits 9bb6cef and 3b15a16 touch none of the patched files, and the patches apply there too); `scripts/` the new helpers. `../RUN.md` runs the lane with them.

| # | problem (as seen) | fix | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VC3D's moving `latest` release (baf5e60, 2026-11-03) dropped `vc_render_tifxyz --prefetch-remote`, which the lane passes on every render, and `setup.sh` fell back to `latest` (seen before a render ran) | `setup.sh` pins the archived c9f8c57 build; no fallback to `latest` | renders of the PHerc0343 First Letters segment bit-identical to the published ones (layer and valid-mask sha256 equal the claim's sidecar) |
| 2 | the 0fb38c45 fitter imports `vc3d_fiber_format`, not shipped with `scripts/spiral` | `scripts/box_prep.sh` adds `vesuvius/src/vc3d_fiber_format` next to the fitter | fitter imports and runs |
| 3 | on an anticlockwise fit (PHerc0175A) the published crossings cache (`*.dbm.crossings.npz`) "could not be remapped ... walk crossing partner is out of range", and the exact rebuild then rejects the mirrored coordinates ("track coordinates must lie in [0, 2**20)"): the fit dies | `erwin_data.sh` no longer fetches the cache; the fitter computes exact crossings natively (7,438,580 events in ~1 min) | three ACW bands of PHerc0175A fitted to 30,000 steps |
| 4 | a cut 8.5 GB tracks download (backgrounded `curl` + bare `wait`, which returns 0 whatever the children did) passed the data stage; the fitter then fell back to the placeholder `.dbm` ("database disk image is malformed") | `erwin_data.sh` resumes each file until its size equals the server's Content-Length and fails the stage otherwise; tracks can come from an rsync mirror first (`TRACKS_MIRROR`) | truncated `coordinates.i32` (5.97 of 8.48 GB) completed by range resume, all 9 files size-checked |
| 5 | triage (`tri_rank.py`) reloaded the winding's full fused map and ran `gap_stats` (2 mm blur) over the whole winding for every window: 46 min for 40 windings of PHerc0175A on a 24-core host | map cached per winding; `gap_stats(cols=)` computes on the window +- 1000 px (> 4 sigma of the widest blur; everything outside the window is masked anyway) | identical output on 6 windows of 3 windings (`a == b` for every returned field), 2-3x faster on the same host; the triage stage took 2,792 s for 213 windows before the fix and 1,776 s / 1,444 s for 681 / 680 windows after (other hosts; lane timing logs) |
| 6 | (addition) `window_score.py` computed the row score twice per window and scored windings one at a time | one `score()` call reused by the alignment branch; `WS_PROCS` process pool over windings (threads split across processes in `lane_box.sh`) | before: not timed; after, `WS_PROCS=6`: 120 windings scored while the rest rendered and inked (53-67 min post stage), with 112-290 s of scoring left after it (lane timing logs) |
| 7 | (addition) Reader v3 was not among the lane's ink models | Reader v3 in the lane, its batch capped at 8 for its 384 px tiles; `INK_MODELS` selects models and weights (e.g. `rv3:1`) | Reader v3 alone inks a winding in a median 4 s of model time (240 windings logged, max 9.5 s) |
| 8 | results were only on the rented box; a box stopped by its host came back wiped | `scripts/box_sync.sh`: durable outputs (fit, meshes, results, logs) to off-box storage every 10 min, following links (`out/*/fitted` are symlinks into `fits/`), and a check that the fit checkpoint landed | after the fix: fit checkpoint verified off-box during the run |
| 9 | 8 of the 20 eligible scrolls have no Lasagna normals, and the lane's `NORMALS=syn` branch called a `make_normals.py` it did not ship (the authors added theirs in 9bb6cef, 2026-11-08) | `../normals/make_normals.py` (CPU), called by the `erwin_data.sh` patch on the bucket's m7 | three ACW bands of PHerc0175A fitted; the PHerc0343 control end to end with these normals ranks the published text first (`../RUN.md`) |
| 10 | (found in review; not hit in our runs) the post stage's gate compared inked windings with *rendered* ones, and `one_winding` returns 0 on a missing snap, a failed flatten or a failed render, so a band with 1 of 120 planned windings rendered and inked passed as complete | `s_post` counts against the planned windings (`windings.txt`), writes `planned / rendered / inked` and the missing windings to `lane.log` (packed with the results), and runs a second render pass that retries what the first left; the 80 % tolerance stays as upstream had it | `scripts/test_post_gate.sh` (snap, render and ink stubbed, 10 planned): 1 of 10 rendered passes before the fix (rc 0) and fails after (rc 1); a render that fails once is retried; every run we report rendered and inked all its planned windings (40, 120, 120, 40) |

`patches/` covers 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 (and `lane_box.sh` for 6 and 10, `ink_lane.py`/`fastpipe.py` for 7).
